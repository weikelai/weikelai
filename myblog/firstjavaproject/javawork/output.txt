In the quiet town of Riverstone, the early morning light spilled gently over the rooftops.
Birds drifted above the trees, and a soft breeze carried the scent of fresh grass.
People began to wake, unaware that this day would bring something unexpected.

At the edge of the town stood an old library, its wooden sign faded by time.
Inside, shelves were filled with books of every size and color.
Among them was a dusty blue volume that had not been opened for decades.

Evelyn, the young caretaker, noticed the book while cleaning.
Its cover felt unusually warm, as if it carried a story longing to be told.
When she opened it, a bright golden glow filled the room.

The words on the first page shifted like ripples on water, rearranging themselves into new sentences.
They spoke of forgotten kingdoms, ancient travelers, and a path hidden beneath Riverstone itself.
Evelyn blinked, wondering if she was dreaming.

But the glow did not fade.
Instead, it swirled into the shape of a small door—glimmering, delicate, and inviting.
With a steady breath, Evelyn reached out and stepped into the unknown.
